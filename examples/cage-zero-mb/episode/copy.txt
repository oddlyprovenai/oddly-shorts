A personal dashboard that drops to 0 MB when closed

Cage runs on 127.0.0.1 only, encrypts ~/.cage with AES-256-GCM + scrypt, uses ~20 MB locked / ~23 MB open, and exits to 0 MB when idle. Link in @oddlyprovenai bio.

Sources: https://github.com/oddlyprovenai/cage
AI-assisted animation: synthesized voice, music and sound effects.
