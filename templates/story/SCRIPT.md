## Narration
The mightiest warship in Sweden sank before it even left the harbour. Stockholm, 1628. The king wanted the Vasa to be the strongest ship afloat, so he ordered seventy-two heavy guns. Lots more. A second gun deck went on top. Before she sailed, they tested her stability. Thirty sailors ran from side to side across the deck. She rolled so hard the admiral stopped the test after three runs. And then they launched her anyway. About 1,300 metres out, a gust tipped her over. Water poured in through the open gunports, and down she went. Luckily, the cold Baltic kept her safe. Unluckily, it kept her for 333 years. It's like a bookshelf that wobbles in front of everyone, and you still fill the top shelf. Today, a failed stability test stops the launch. And the Vasa? She sits in a museum in Stockholm. Divers raised her in 1961. Finally, completely stable. In our lab, our first medicine model failed its test too, so we didn't ship it. The second one passed.
